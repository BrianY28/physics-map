1. Gravitational waves: the concepts are dated. Replace "Binary black hole merger" with "GW170817 neutron-star merger": in 2017 LIGO/Virgo and about 70 observatories saw the same merger in gravitational waves and light (a kilonova), launching multi-messenger astronomy. Replace "Gravitational-wave strain" with "Nanohertz background": pulsar timing arrays (NANOGrav and others, 2023) found evidence for a gravitational-wave background.
2. Schwarzschild solution: the horizon was not understood as a one-way surface until Finkelstein (1958). Do not say the 1916 solution "reveals an event horizon" without that caveat.
