CONTENT ACCURACY

Use Motte's 1729 wording exactly: "Every body perseveres in its state of rest, or of uniform motion in a right line, unless it is compelled to change that state by forces impress'd thereon." Keep the attribution "Andrew Motte's translation, 1729".

Keep your correct point that the Sun's weak gravity is the main remaining force, so the motion is nearly, not exactly, uniform.
